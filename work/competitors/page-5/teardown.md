- **Mechanism:** "two targeted solutions", "works from the inside out" plus "instant relief, cooling sensation". Thin but understandable.
- **Ingredients:** Not present. **Benefits:** emotive.
- **Objections:** weak. No FAQ, no guarantee, no delivery details shown on page.
- **Authority:** "NAFDAC approved" (no number). Video as the main explainer.
- **Testimonials:** 4 **WhatsApp chat screenshots** (the most believable format in the set).
- **Before/after:** Not present.
