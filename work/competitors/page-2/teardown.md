## C. Persuasion
- **Story:** a testimonial told as a story ("Adewale, Lagos") with the full escalation arc. It is the best-written element on the page.
- **Agitation:** very heavy. Bleeding, anaemia, pus, fistula, loss of bowel control, surgery.
- **Mechanism:** nearly none. The claim is "targets the root cause", with no explanation.
- **Ingredients:** Not present. No names, no dosage.
- **Benefits vs features:** a benefits-only list with absolute claims: "Effectively stops rectal bleeding", "eliminating piles completely", "Permanent Solution", "No harmful side effects", "Clinically Proven".
- **Objections:** barely handled (a 30-day guarantee and POD). No FAQ.
- **Authority:** "Medically Approved ✅" and "recommended by healthcare professionals", with no names. **No NAFDAC number on the page.** A stock image stands in for the doctor.
- **Testimonials:** 4 text quotes (names, towns, "Via WhatsApp" with dates) with **placeholder images** (the Elementor default placeholder is showing), so they look generic. Plus the story testimonial near the top.
- **Before/after:** Not present.
- **Comparisons:** surgery is the foil ("without surgery").

## D. Offer
- **3 packages:** ₦25,000 (1 capsule + 1 balm), ₦38,500 (2 + 2), ₦63,500 (3 + 1 extra set). Delivery free. The ₦38,500 card is labelled "Perfect Treatment, Get THIS", and ₦25,000 is labelled "Not Recommended" in the form.
- **Anchor price:** none (no strike-through).
- **Bonus:** "balm free" is the framing. The balm is part of the combo, so this is a pseudo-bonus.
- **Guarantee:** "30 days guarantee" printed on each card, with no terms.
- **Urgency:** three countdown timers, a "Sold 2,836 pcs 85%" stock bar, and a banner reading "(UPDATED: 17 November 2026…) Order before 11:59pm, November 16, 2026 & get free shipping". That deadline is **a day before the date it displays, so it is auto-generated and fake**.
