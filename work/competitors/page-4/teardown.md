## Advertorial analysis (what we can learn)
- Borrowed credibility works because readers read it as news first. The "Promoted" tag is small.
- Storytelling carries it: a woman, a pastor, a church. The reader meets someone like them before the product.
- Weakness: the order step is a text message, so conversion is lost at the last step.
- **Recommendation:** consider an advertorial pre-sell only after the direct page is proven. Keep our page a direct sales page as briefed. Note that placing a sponsored article on a news site needs the publisher's ad rules and legal approval for health claims.
