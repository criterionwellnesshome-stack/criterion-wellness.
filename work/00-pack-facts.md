## Conflicts & notes
1. **Pack vs our research, product name.** Research calls it "Pile Relief Tea". The pack says **Pile Terminator Tea**. → *Pack wins.* Page uses "Pile Terminator Tea".
2. **Pack vs research, ingredients.** Research says Dongoyaro and Negro pepper. The pack says Olax subscorpioidea and Gongronema latifolium (+ unnamed "other formulas"). → *Pack wins.* The page now shows the pack's wording. **Dongoyaro/Negro pepper are no longer used on the page.** Please confirm the pack is right.
3. **Pack vs research, directions.** Research: 1 teabag, 200 ml, steep 10 minutes, once a day. Pack: boil 5 minutes, leave to cool, **morning and night** before food. → *Pack wins.* The page uses the pack wording.
4. **The pack contradicts itself on dosage.** The front says "Once a Day" (with a sun-and-moon icon) and "30 servings", while the back says "morning and night". Twice a day would make 30 teabags last 15 days, not a month. The page uses the back-panel directions and shows a yellow **[CONFIRM]** note for the frequency. Also the 3-month pack and "about ₦500 a day" price lines assume one bag a day, so **if it is twice a day, those lines need to change.**
5. **Claims printed on the pack that I did not enlarge.** (Rule 4.) "Herbal Tea for Pile Treatment", the name "Terminator", "improve blood flow and reduce inflammation within the gastrointestinal tract", and "Eases pain when passing stool" (stated flatly). On the page I use softer wording ("made to help…"), I do **not** repeat "Treatment" or "Terminator" as a promise, and I **omit** the "improve blood flow / reduce inflammation" line. The product name still appears because it is the name. A regulator/pharmacist check is advisable for the name and tagline.
6. **Pack vs research, benefits:** consistent. The research's benefit lines are the same as the pack's benefit list.
7. **Pack graphic:** it shows a medical anatomy drawing. Our research says no graphic or shaming images. The page shows the pack photo as supplied, but I'd use a real photo of the pack, ideally without the anatomy picture on ads.
8. **NAFDAC:** the pack shows no NAFDAC number, and your website says "application in progress" for other Criterion products, so the page keeps a placeholder and never says "NAFDAC approved".
