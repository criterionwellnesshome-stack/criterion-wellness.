LinkedIn 1: The silent cost of piles at work
We talk about burnout, back pain and bad posture. We rarely talk about piles.

In our research we found one person saying they smile at work and hurt in their seat.
Another said they pray before going to the toilet. A third said nobody understands.

These are your colleagues. They are in your meetings. They sit through long calls and tell
no one.

If you manage people, the first step is normalising the conversation. Provide comfortable
seating. Allow breaks. Do not joke about it.

If you are living with this, you are not alone.

At Criterion Herbaceuticals, we are building honest content and a simple herbal tea for
people living with piles. Follow us, or send us a message if you want to talk.


LinkedIn 2: What a wellness brand owes its customers
A wellness brand should be able to answer five questions: what is inside, how much, how
to use it, who makes it, and how to reach them.

In the piles space, we have seen sellers promise a "permanent cure" and "tested and
trusted" with no label in sight. Customers are tired.

Our answers for Pile Relief Tea: Dongoyaro and Negro pepper, 3 g per teabag, one a day,
made by Criterion Herbaceuticals, +234 816 455 3746.

Transparency is not a feature. It is the minimum.


LinkedIn 3: Why we started with honesty
We could have started with big claims. We started with a research question: what do
people with piles actually say?

We read hundreds of public comments. We saw shame, fear of surgery, money worries and
distrust of sellers. We saw gratitude when someone spoke honestly.

That is why our content starts with the feeling, not the product.
