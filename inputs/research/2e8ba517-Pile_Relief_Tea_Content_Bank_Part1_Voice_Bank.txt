Rules for using this part

        Quotes can be used as inspiration, as a pull-quote on a poster, or as a prompt for a
        post. If you use one publicly, anonymise it and do not tag the person.
        Never pass a quote off as a Criterion customer. It is from the public internet.
        Ideas marked Our idea are suggestions, not facts.

D. Money
        "I do not have enough money to undergo the surgery yet." (YouTube comment)
        "The money wasted can't just be mentioned." (Nairaland thread opener, on drugs and
        alagbo)
        A Nairaland poster said a hospital asked him to do a scan costing about N30,000.
        A recovered Nairaland poster listed his routine: bitter leaf juice morning and evening,
        Daflon (he said N1,500 for 15 tablets), Anusol suppository, a Chinese product bought
        on Jumia for N2,500.
        Google suggestions in Nigeria: "piles surgery cost in nigeria", "how much is pile surgery
        in nigeria", "pile operation cost in nigeria", "best medicine for piles in nigeria".

What this tells us: People count the cost of every step. Price will be judged against drugs
and the scan, not against tea. Talk about "one teabag a day for a month" in plain naira
terms once the price is set.

Our ideas:

        A post: "What piles have cost you so far. Add it up."
        A comparison table post (not price-shaming): common spending (drugs, creams, scan,
        agbo, lost workdays) vs a month of Pile Relief Tea.
